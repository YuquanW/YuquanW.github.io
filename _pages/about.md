Hello! I'm Yuquan, a former Ph.D. student in the Department of Biostatistics at Fudan University. During my Ph.D., my primary research interests focused on **Mendelian randomization (MR)** and **polygenetic risk score (PRS)**. Currently, I’m expanding my skill set in bioinformatics, particularly in analyzing single-cell and spatial transcriptomics data. I'm especially interested in integrating whole-genome sequencing, single-cell data, and phenotypic information to perform causal inference in observational studies in the near future.
